# STAR FORGE: Sector 1 — The Physics of Information

A single-file 3D learning game (`index.html`). Pilot a small starship along a glowing star-path across five planets. Each planet teaches one mental model that inventors use:

1. **First-Principles Decomposition** (Cyan Crystal Planet)
2. **Shannon Surprisal (Information as Unexpectedness)** (Gold & Violet Pulsar Planet)
3. **Kolmogorov Complexity (Compression as Intelligence)** (Emerald Fractal Planet)
4. **The Idiot Index (Atom-to-Product Ratio)** (Magma & Chrome Forge Planet)
5. **Thermodynamic Entropy in Organizations** (Neon Purple & Amber Nebula Planet)

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser while online. Three.js r128, the Tailwind Play CDN and Google Fonts load from CDNs. You can also serve the folder with any static server, for example `npx serve .`.

- Click or tap an unlocked planet, or use **◀ Previous / Next Planet ▶**, to fly there.
- Drag to orbit the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
- Keyboard: `←` `→` fly between planets, `Enter` lands, `M` toggles the sector map, `Esc` closes panels.

## What's inside

- **5-stage loop on every planet:** a curiosity bet with a confidence boost, an interactive canvas physics toy with an Applied vs. Violated toggle, active recall plus a "why?" question, a dual-mode Star Card forge (tap-to-snap or your own words), and mastery with a Cosmic Chest ship upgrade.
- **Positive-only economy:** Stardust is never taken away. A Mission Combo (1x/2x/3x/5x) grows with each win, and wrong answers never break it.
- **Supercharge Orbit:** mastered planets glow gold after 60 seconds for a 10-second, 2x-Stardust review. The gap doubles after each review.
- **Sector Boss, Hyperdrive Replay:** a 15-second mind-palace pause, then a reverse chain from planet 5 to 1, then a rapid-fire forward chain that pays a +1,000 Stardust jackpot.
- **Procedural Web Audio:** a 104 BPM D Dorian "curious shop" loop (marimba pluck, walking bass, celesta with a delay tail) and synthesized sound effects.
- **Saved progress:** stored in `localStorage` (key `starforge.sector1.v1`). Reset it from ⚙️ Settings.
